Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PrimeTimePix ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website and services (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your username, email address, and password (stored in hashed form).
  • Gameplay data — the leagues you join, the picks you make, and your standings.
  • Communications preferences — whether you have opted in or out of reminder emails.
  • Technical data — basic log and device information generated automatically when you use the Service.

How we use your information

  • Operate the Service — create your account, run leagues, grade picks, and display standings.
  • Send transactional email such as account, league, and password-reset messages.
  • Send optional pick-reminder emails, which you can turn off at any time (see below).
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the Service.

Email preferences & unsubscribing

Reminder emails are optional. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of any reminder email, or by turning off "Email reminders" in your profile settings. We will still send essential transactional messages (for example, password resets).

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that help us operate the Service (for example, our email delivery and hosting providers), and only as needed to provide the Service. Your username and standings are visible to other members of the leagues you join.

Data retention & your choices

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You may request that we correct or delete your account information by contacting us. Deleting your account removes your personal information from active use, subject to any records we must keep for legal or security reasons.

Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@primetimepixsports.com.

PrimeTimePix is an independent, fan-operated entertainment platform and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the NFL, any NFL team, ESPN, or any other league or broadcaster.
